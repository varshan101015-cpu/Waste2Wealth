About

Why this app?

Construction waste fills landfills worldwide. Waste2Wealth gives small contractors free, instant sorting instructions via their phone.

Quick Tips

  • Concrete: Remove rebar first, then crush
  • Bricks: Clean off mortar for better reuse
  • Wood: Keep treated and untreated separate
  • Metal: Magnets separate steel from aluminum
