Smart Segregation

Use AI-assisted identification to classify C&D waste such as concrete, bricks, metal, wood and other materials, then recommend the appropriate recovery stream.

AI-assisted · instant

Point, speak or type. The AI sorts the pile into streams — concrete aggregate, brick, metal scrap, timber and non-recyclables — and routes each one to the right recovery channel.

Material Recovery

Recover and reprocess concrete, bricks, metals and other suitable materials so they return to construction as new aggregate or reusable elements.

Crush · clean · reuse

Concrete becomes recycled aggregate and road base, clean brick becomes reusable brick or brick aggregate, and metal lines up for the scrap yard — closing the loop on the job site.

Energy Recovery

Suitable non-recyclable waste is directed toward responsible energy-recovery processes instead of unnecessary landfill disposal.

Divert from landfill

What can't be recycled — clean wood residuals, sorted fines and non-reusable fractions — is routed to licensed energy-from-waste facilities, keeping it out of the ground.

Impact Tracking

See measurable outcomes: waste diverted from landfill, materials recovered, estimated CO₂ avoided and energy recovered where applicable.

Live metrics

Every batch updates diverted tonnes, recovered tonnes and the CO₂ and energy savings your site is delivering — proof that sorting pays.
